## Full Description

Master Chinese character stroke order with Hanzi Dictionary & Strokes — the offline-first learning app built for HSK preparation.

**Flashcards with Spaced Repetition**
Review vocabulary smarter, not harder. The built-in SM-2 algorithm schedules flashcard reviews at optimal intervals so you retain characters long-term. Choose any HSK level, track your progress, and watch your mastery grow.

**Stroke Order Practice**
See exactly how each character is written. Animated stroke demos, interactive practice with real-time feedback, and calligraphy templates help you develop proper writing technique. Practice with grid guides or freehand.

**HSK 1–7 Courses**
3,090 characters organized by HSK level. Follow guided courses from HSK 1 basics through advanced HSK 7 content. Track your streak, completion rate, and daily practice history.

**14,000+ Offline Dictionary**
Search by character, pinyin, or meaning. The built-in dictionary works entirely offline — look up any character instantly without an internet connection. Save favorites and review your search history.

**Daily Widget & Reminders**
Pin the home screen widget for quick access to the daily character. Set optional daily reminders to build a consistent study habit.

**Privacy First**
Your data stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking. Practice history, settings, and flashcard progress are stored locally. Optional in-app purchase for HSK 2–7 flashcard access.
